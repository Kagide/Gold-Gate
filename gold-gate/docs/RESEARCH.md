# Research and design rationale

17 November 2026. Primary business source: supplied two-page Gold Gate flyer, both pages text-extracted; page two visually inspected, with the logo extracted from the PDF.

## What the flyer verifies

Gold Gate describes itself as a multidisciplinary private limited liability company serving business and personal clients from Kigali. It lists accounting, taxation, business advisory, IT and engineering support. Its listed address is Kigali City Tower, 14th floor, and its telephone is (+250) 788301812. These are verified as statements in the flyer, not independently verified current company details. The tagline is “The difference is real”. No email, social profile, confirmed WhatsApp number, staff identities, qualifications, founding date or customer results were supplied.

Five editorial categories organise the flyer’s offerings. IT and marketing combine offerings that the flyer distributes across start-up, support and systems sections. It is an editorial grouping, not a claimed legal division.

## Reference-site review

Public page structures and content retrieved using web research. This was a structural review, not a full interactive or mobile audit of competitors. Country redirects were observed for PwC and Deloitte Digital. Findings below distinguish observed structure from the design decisions it informed.

| Reference | Observed structure | Transferable decision for Gold Gate |
|---|---|---|
| https://www.bdo.global/en-gb/home | Prominent editorial stories, contact, locations and career entry points. | A clear opening message and prominent inquiry journey. Avoid an insight-heavy homepage until real editorial material exists. |
| https://kpmg.com/xx/en.html | Skip navigation, global selector, business-priority stories and topic pathways. | Explicit accessible navigation, scannable topics and clear information hierarchy. No accounts or country controls without a real purpose. |
| https://www.pwc.com/us/en.html | Layered navigation for services, industries, research, company and technology. | Group five services in a concise disclosure menu. Give each service a dedicated landing page. |
| https://www.deloittedigital.com/it/en.html | Capability-led navigation and a contact form with topic, business contact fields and privacy acknowledgement. | Tie the inquiry to a service, use deliberate typography, and maintain a direct conversion path. |

These sources do not validate Gold Gate’s credentials. No competitor copy, branding or distinctive layouts were reproduced. Public HTML summaries do not establish competitors’ precise animation, photography quality, keyboard operation or mobile performance.

## Rwanda business context

Official Rwanda Development Board sources present business procedures and investment facilitation as distinct areas. This supports a visitor journey that starts with the client’s stage and directs international entrants to planning, registration assistance and operating systems. The website deliberately offers no registration timeline, investment eligibility threshold or tax-rate advice.

Sources reviewed:
- https://businessprocedures.rdb.rw/menu/25?l=en
- https://rdb.rw/one-stop-centre/
- https://rdb.rw/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Service-Charter-ENGLISH.pdf

Design inference: local entrepreneurs need a comprehensible starting point, existing businesses need direct routes to accounts and tax support, and international visitors need a clear Rwanda entry point and a credible way to ask questions. These are proposed visitor journeys, not findings from Gold Gate customer interviews.

## Art direction

Deep navy anchors the experience. Warm gold marks actions and details; a darker gold variant carries small text for contrast. White and light gray create rhythm. Open geometric gates recall the company name without copying the source flyer’s circular text arrangement. A single illustrative architectural photo supplies scale and perspective. Native system sans-serif keeps type loading fast, with a restrained Georgia italic accent. Original raster logo and exact tagline retain identity.

The homepage progresses from positioning to company introduction, the five services, an interactive finder, a proposed engagement conversation, international entry and a final inquiry action. Internal pages prioritise readable service detail. Motion stays limited to short hover transitions, with reduced-motion overrides.

## Content decisions requiring approval

- The owner should approve the English editorial rewrite and five-category grouping.
- Excluded as current advice: inheritance/capital-gains specifics, foreign “customs and excise” terminology, tax protection insurance and outdated CSR references.
- “Binding document preparation” is ambiguous in the flyer and is not promoted pending clarification.
- Retained broad RRA enquiry support, VAT administration and personal tax inquiry services, with current-law review required.
- The three-step engagement description is a proposed website journey, not a verified contractual service process.
