# Owner demonstration

Allow 5–7 minutes. Run the site locally with `npm ci` and `npm run dev`. No live account or paid service is required.

1. Open Home. Explain the business problem: prospective clients need one credible place to understand Gold Gate’s services and begin a conversation. Point out the original logo/tagline, service navigation and contact action.
2. Local entrepreneur: choose “I want to start a business” in the finder. Open Business Start-Up, review planning and registration support, then “Discuss this service”. Confirm the form selects Business Start-Up. Demonstrate validation using empty fields. After the owner configures email, demonstrate an actual approved inquiry and verify inbox delivery.
3. International business: return Home and show the Rwanda entry section. Follow “Discuss your plans in Rwanda”, explaining that the service is an enquiry about planning, registration and operating setup, not a guarantee of approvals or tax outcomes.
4. Established company: select the tax or accounting choice. Open the corresponding page and follow the service-specific contact action. Show the optional company/phone fields and the privacy acknowledgement.
5. At mobile width, open the menu and Services disclosure. Close with Escape and show that the content remains reachable by keyboard. Show the direct telephone link.
6. Conclude with owner approvals: contact/email, service/legal review, logo vector and imagery, privacy and deployment. Current review mode cannot send real inquiries. The error state says this explicitly.

Business value is a design intention: clearer discovery, more credible presentation, fewer steps to a relevant inquiry, and an architecture that can accept approved team and article content later. Do not promise conversion rates, revenue or search positions.
